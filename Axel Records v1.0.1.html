    Library Controls

    • W / A / S / D — move around the hall
    • Arrow keys — browse records; Enter inspects the highlighted one
    • Space / Esc — flip a record / return to the library
    • F / P / R / ? — favorite, play preview, random pick, or show shortcuts
    • E / Q — move up / down
    • Drag — look around; drag a record along its tier to reorder it, or click to inspect
    • Shelf tabs — jump to a bookcase; double-click to rename
    • Sort records — arrange by name, artist, genre, or year
    • Use Move to shelf in album details to organize records into any bookcase.
